AI Prompt: Qualitative Analysis of APP Wellbeing and Belonging Interviews
SECTION 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW
You are working on a quality improvement project focused on Advanced Practice Provider (APP) Wellbeing and Belonging. A series of 13 interviews was conducted across 5 different ICUs within the same facility, representing 3 different teams.
Background: Critical care APPs demonstrated low well-being and belonging scores in a institutional survey. This QI project aims to identify actionable changes that can improve well-being and/or belonging for these teams.
Important Scope: Your analysis should focus on identifying shared issues across the organization and system among Critical Care APP Teams. This is not a project focused on unit-specific or team-specific issues. Look for patterns that transcend individual units and people.
SECTION 2: ANALYTICAL APPROACH
Methodology
Use deductive thematic analysis based on three predetermined themes identified in the literature review:
•	Mentorship and Professional Development
•	Professional Autonomy and Role Clarity
•	Organizational Support and Recognition
Coding Process
•	Review each transcript systematically for content related to the three predetermined themes
•	Develop specific codes (sub-categories) under each theme that emerge from the interview data
•	Codes should capture specific issues, experiences, or perspectives that APPs describe
•	A single quote may relate to multiple themes or codes
Quality Criteria
•	Each code should be supported by data from at least 3 different interviews
•	Themes should demonstrate clear patterns across the dataset
•	Note if certain codes appear with particular frequency or intensity across interviews
•	Flag any contradictory perspectives within themes (e.g., if some APPs experience an issue differently)
•	Ensure quotes are representative of the broader pattern, not outliers
Data Handling
•	Use verbatim quotes but may use [...] to indicate removed content for brevity or clarity
•	Remove all identifying information including names, specific unit details, or other identifiers
•	Indicate which interview number each quote comes from
•	If a quote bridges two related ideas, you may include the full quote under both relevant codes
SECTION 3: DELIVERABLES
Deliverable #1: Organized Quotes by Theme and Code
Format: Microsoft Word document
Structure:
•	Organize by the three main themes
•	Under each theme, list codes (sub-categories) that emerged from your analysis
•	Under each code, provide 3-5 representative quotes that illustrate that code
•	Each quote should be followed by the interview number in this format: "– Interview #X"
Example Format:
THEME 1: MENTORSHIP AND PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
Code: Inadequate Onboarding Support
"Quote illustrating lack of structured onboarding..." – Interview #3
"Quote about feeling unprepared for role..." – Interview #7
"Quote describing gaps in initial training..." – Interview #11
Code: Limited Access to Continuing Education
"Quote about barriers to conference attendance..." – Interview #2
Requirements:
•	Include 3-5 representative quotes per code minimum
•	Quotes should be verbatim with [...] used only for brevity
•	Remove any identifying information
•	Ensure quotes clearly demonstrate the code they're illustrating
Deliverable #2: Major Takeaways
Format: Microsoft Word document with bulleted list
Structure:
•	Organize by the three main themes
•	Under each theme, provide bulleted major takeaways from your analysis
•	Each takeaway should be 1-2 sentences
•	Clearly indicate how the takeaway connects to wellbeing, belonging, or both
•	Include indication of prevalence (e.g., "across multiple teams," "consistently mentioned," "nearly universal")
Example Format:
THEME 1: MENTORSHIP AND PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
•	APPs across multiple ICUs reported lack of structured mentorship beyond initial orientation, leading to feelings of isolation and uncertainty that directly impact sense of belonging (mentioned in 9/13 interviews)
•	Professional development opportunities are perceived as limited or difficult to access due to scheduling and workload constraints, reducing sense of institutional investment in APP growth and wellbeing (consistently mentioned across all teams)
Requirements:
•	Each takeaway should synthesize multiple data points, not single quotes
•	Connect takeaways explicitly to wellbeing and/or belonging
•	Indicate scope/prevalence when possible
•	Focus on shared experiences across interviews, not individual perspectives
Deliverable #3: Prioritized Recommendations
Format: Microsoft Word document
Structure:
•	Organize recommendations by priority level (High, Medium, Lower)
•	Recommendations should be institutional/system-level, not unit-specific
•	Each recommendation should include:
• Recommendation statement (specific, actionable change)
• Supporting evidence (cite themes/codes and reference interview data)
• Expected impact (explain how this addresses wellbeing and/or belonging)
• Connection to theme(s) (identify which of the 3 themes this addresses)
Prioritization Framework
Consider these factors when prioritizing:
•	Potential impact on wellbeing and/or belonging (based on frequency and intensity in interviews)
•	Feasibility of implementation at institutional/system level
•	Breadth of issue across interviews and teams
•	Alignment with existing evidence-based practices for APP wellbeing
Example Format:
HIGH PRIORITY RECOMMENDATIONS
Recommendation #1: Implement a Structured 12-Month Mentorship Program for All New Critical Care APPs
Supporting Evidence:
•	Falls under "Mentorship and Professional Development" theme
•	9/13 interviews mentioned lack of ongoing mentorship beyond orientation
•	APPs described feeling "abandoned" after initial onboarding (Interviews #2, #5, #8, #11)
•	Current informal mentorship is inconsistent and depends on individual unit culture
Expected Impact:
This structured approach would directly address belonging by creating consistent professional relationships and support systems. APPs would have designated mentors for guidance on clinical questions, role navigation, and professional integration, reducing isolation and uncertainty that undermine both wellbeing and belonging.
Requirements:
•	Recommendations must be specific and actionable (not vague like "improve communication")
•	Include 5-8 total recommendations across priority levels
•	Each recommendation should cite specific evidence from your thematic analysis
•	Focus on changes that address root causes identified in themes, not just symptoms
•	Ensure recommendations are feasible at the institutional/system level
SECTION 4: ANALYTICAL NOTES
Your Role: This analysis serves as an initial qualitative review that will be validated by the research team. Focus on clear pattern identification and descriptive analysis of how interview data maps to the three predetermined themes. Your interpretation should stay grounded in the data while identifying meaningful patterns.
Handling Contradictions: If you encounter contradictory perspectives (e.g., some APPs feel supported while others don't regarding the same issue), note this in your analysis. This may indicate variability in implementation or experience that warrants attention.
Depth of Analysis: Go beyond simple categorization. Consider:
•	How do issues in one theme interact with or exacerbate issues in another?
•	What underlying organizational structures or cultures might explain patterns?
•	How do these issues specifically impact wellbeing versus belonging (or both)?
If Data is Insufficient: If you find limited or no data supporting one of the three predetermined themes, note this explicitly. It may indicate that theme is less relevant to this APP population or that interview questions didn't adequately probe that area.
